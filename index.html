About

From the kitchen floor to the coaching room

I've been where you are. I've run the passes, managed the teams, absorbed the pressure, and learned — sometimes the hard way — what it actually takes to lead people in this industry.

After three decades on the front line, from crew member to general manager at some of the UK's most demanding establishments, I stepped back to answer a bigger question: why does hospitality keep losing its best people?

The answer wasn't complicated. It was leadership. And so My Mental Kitchen was born — a consultancy dedicated to transforming how hospitality leaders show up for their teams, every single day.

During 2019 & 2020, I certified as a transformational coach with a foundation in psychodynamic psychotherapy and counselling and qualified as a Mental Health First Aider.
