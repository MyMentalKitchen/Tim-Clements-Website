- The Fat Duck repeating is the single most powerful proof point. A 3 Michelin Star restaurant does not repeat a programme that doesn't deliver.
- Mc & Sons proves versatility: the programme works across fine dining AND multi-site pub operations
- Marketing to men in hospitality: avoid therapy-adjacent language. Frame emotional/psychological work as leadership competency development.
